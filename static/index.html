Strategy Details - What Each Signal Means

Uses 6 different market indicators. When multiple signals fire, the system picks the one with highest priority.

  • 📉 RSI Oversold (Priority: 80)
    Triggers when: Stock is heavily sold and RSI drops below 25
    Plain English: "The stock has been beaten down too much and is likely to bounce back"
  • 📈 RSI Bullish Cross (Priority: 63)
    Triggers when: RSI crosses above 50 from below
    Plain English: "The stock is gaining momentum and starting an upward trend"
  • 📊 Bollinger Band Break (Priority: 80)
    Triggers when: Price touches the lower Bollinger Band
    Plain English: "The stock has moved unusually far from its average and should snap back"
  • 💹 Price Above VWAP (Priority: 57.6)
    Triggers when: Current price is above the volume-weighted average
    Plain English: "Big traders are buying at these levels, showing institutional support"
  • 🔻 ROC Below Threshold (Priority: 78.9)
    Triggers when: Rate of change drops below -1%
    Plain English: "The stock has dropped quickly and is due for a reversal"
  • 🎯 MACD Positive (Priority: 63)
    Triggers when: MACD line crosses above signal line
    Plain English: "Short-term momentum is overtaking long-term trend - bullish signal"
